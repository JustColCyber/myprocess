## Webhooks

webhook.site

Other options:

- Pipedream RequestBin: captures requests and lets you add workflow steps.
- Beeceptor: a mock endpoint that can return custom responses.
- Hookdeck Console: free request inspection with replay.
- smee.io: relays webhooks to a local server, mainly used for GitHub.
- ngrok: tunnels a public URL to a service on your own machine, so you can test your own receiver.
- httpbin.org: echoes back whatever you send.
